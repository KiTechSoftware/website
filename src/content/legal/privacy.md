# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

KiTech Software ("KiTech", "we", "our", or "us") develops software products and services for individuals, organisations, businesses, educational institutions, charities, and governments.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information in a transparent, secure, and lawful manner. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

This Privacy Policy applies to all KiTech Software websites, applications, products, services, APIs, and other online services unless a product-specific privacy notice states otherwise.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all KiTech Software websites, cloud-hosted services, desktop applications, mobile applications, APIs, documentation, and customer support.

## 3. Information We Collect

Depending on the product or service, we may collect information you provide, technical information generated through use, and content you create. Product-specific Privacy Notices describe the data collected by each product.

## 4. Why We Process Information

We process information only where necessary to provide services, authenticate users, protect accounts, improve reliability, provide support, meet legal obligations, process payments, and deliver requested functionality.

## 5. Legal Basis

Where applicable, we rely on consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interests, legal obligations, or protection of vital interests.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only when necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, protect users, support business transfers, or with your consent.

## 7. Security

We employ appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption where appropriate, access controls, audit logging, secure development practices, and vulnerability management.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Where information is transferred between jurisdictions, appropriate safeguards are implemented in accordance with applicable law.

## 9. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and enforce agreements.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Our products are not directed at children unless explicitly stated.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating the version number and effective date.

## 13. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed using the contact information published in our Contact Policy.
