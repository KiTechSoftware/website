# Subprocessors and Service Providers

## 1. Scope

This page identifies material third-party providers currently used to operate the public KiTech Software website.

It is not a complete list for every KiTech product. Product-specific privacy notices and contractual documentation will identify additional processors, subprocessors, hosting providers, integrations, or regional infrastructure where applicable.

## 2. Public website providers

| Provider | Service | Purpose | Information that may be processed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub, Inc. | GitHub repositories, Actions, and Pages | Source control, automated builds, and static website hosting | Repository contributor information, workflow logs, IP address, browser and request metadata associated with website delivery |
| Google LLC | Google Fonts | Delivery of website font files | IP address, browser headers, requested font resources, and ordinary connection metadata |

We do not use these providers to sell personal information or run behavioural advertising on the public website.

## 3. Product-specific providers

A KiTech product may use different infrastructure depending on:

- whether the service is KiTech-hosted, partner-operated, customer-managed, or self-hosted;
- the selected region or data-residency arrangement;
- enabled integrations and optional features;
- payment, communications, authentication, monitoring, support, or security requirements; and
- contractual or regulatory obligations.

A product-specific notice should identify providers that materially process personal information for that product.

## 4. Provider assessment

Where KiTech appoints a processor or subprocessor, we assess matters proportionate to the service and risk, which may include:

- security and privacy controls;
- data location and transfer arrangements;
- confidentiality and access restrictions;
- incident notification and cooperation;
- deletion and return of information;
- audit or assurance information; and
- contractual data-protection obligations.

## 5. International transfers

A provider may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law restricts a transfer, KiTech uses an appropriate transfer mechanism or lawful exception as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/).

## 6. Changes

We will update this page when a material public-website provider is added, removed, or changes role.

Product customers may receive separate notice of material subprocessor changes where required by contract.
