Who we are

A software company, not a conventional agency.

We do not operate as a general-purpose development shop. Our work is focused on products we own, ventures we support, and long-term partnerships where technology is central to the business.

That focus allows us to make stronger architectural decisions, invest in maintainability, and build systems that can evolve over years rather than only reach a launch date.

We're also active supporters of open source, and maintain a number of tools publicly on GitHub.
